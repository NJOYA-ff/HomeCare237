Introduction

HomeCare237 ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use the HomeCare237 mobile and web application.

By using HomeCare237, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, and password (encrypted).
  • Health data: Medical history, diagnoses, vitals, medications, and appointment details that you enter or that your healthcare provider records.
  • Profile information: Blood type, allergies, emergency contact, and insurance details.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and app interactions to improve the service.
  • Device data: Device type, OS version, and push notification tokens (for reminders).

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and personalise the HomeCare237 service.
  • Connect you with healthcare professionals and health units.
  • Send appointment reminders, health alerts, and emergency notifications.
  • Improve app features, security, and performance.
  • Comply with applicable health and data protection laws.

We do not sell your personal or health data to third parties.

Health Data & HIPAA / GDPR

HomeCare237 handles health data with the highest level of care. All health records are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We comply with applicable data protection regulations, including GDPR principles for users in the EU and equivalent local legislation in Cameroon.

Your health data is only accessible to you and healthcare professionals you have explicitly authorised within the app.

Data Sharing

We share your data only in these limited circumstances:

  • Healthcare providers: Doctors and health units you connect with through the app.
  • Service providers: Firebase (Google), Twilio (messaging), and other infrastructure partners who are bound by data processing agreements.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law or to protect the safety of users.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • End-to-end encrypted communications for consultations.
  • Biometric and PIN lock for app access.
  • Firebase Authentication for secure sign-in.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Export your health records in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app or by email. Continued use of HomeCare237 after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
